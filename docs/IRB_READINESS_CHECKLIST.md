## Expert validation

- [ ] Number and qualifications of experts justified.
- [ ] Recruitment channel and inclusion criteria defined.
- [ ] Consent/waiver and compensation documented.
- [ ] Rating instrument and synthetic examples attached.
- [ ] Expert names separated from response data where possible.
- [ ] Retention and withdrawal process specified.

## CAP-Data participant study

- [ ] Prospective protocol, hypotheses, outcomes, and analysis plan finalized.
- [ ] A priori sample-size method specified.
- [ ] Synthetic stimuli reviewed for distress and cultural appropriateness.
- [ ] Consent, debrief, withdrawal, compensation, and contact procedures drafted.
- [ ] Optional demographics minimized and separately justified.
- [ ] Logging excludes names, contact information, and raw corpus records.
- [ ] Data retention, access, and deletion documented.
- [ ] Arabic/English materials validated as applicable.

## ReproLogic and depositor contact

- [ ] Depositor-contact template uses neutral discrepancy language.
- [ ] No automated misconduct inference.
- [ ] Publication process allows correction, version mismatch, weighting, and
      rounding explanations.

## PPRL study

- [ ] Data are synthetic or explicitly authorized for linkage evaluation.
- [ ] Ground-truth key is inaccessible to the matcher and human reviewers.
- [ ] Disclosure budget, stopping rules, and review permissions are approved.
- [ ] Live identity lookup is explicitly prohibited.

## Manuscript reporting

- [ ] Ethics statement matches the written institutional determination.
- [ ] Dataset DOI/licence citations included.
- [ ] Funding, conflicts, author contributions, and AI/tool-use disclosure included.
- [ ] No participant-level examples or free-text quotations included.
